# Vision and scope

## Problem

Sports bettors see prices from bookmakers but rarely receive transparent,
reproducible evidence explaining whether a price has value. Many tip products
publish only wins, hide losses and make claims that cannot be audited.

## Product promise

Provide probability estimates and value-bet signals with a permanent audit
trail: model version, data timestamp, market price, estimated probability and
eventual outcome.

## MVP customer

An adult football follower who understands that betting involves risk and
wants analytical support rather than guaranteed picks.

## MVP scope

- Pre-match football only.
- A small set of leagues with reliable statistics and odds.
- 1X2 and over/under markets first.
- Daily signals with model probability, implied probability and expected
  value.
- Historical performance including every published signal.
- Responsible-gambling notices and age restrictions.
- No custody of money, bet acceptance or automatic bet placement.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Operating a sportsbook.
- Promising income or guaranteed returns.
- Redistributing raw third-party data without contractual permission.
- Browser automation against bookmaker websites.
- In-play betting until timestamped historical odds are licensed and validated.
